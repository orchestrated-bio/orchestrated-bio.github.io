One finding

What changed downstream of tolvaptan’s target?

Tolvaptan blocks the V2 receptor in the kidney’s water-handling pathway. We reanalyzed public kidney-expression data from 12 treated and 12 untreated male Pkd1-knockout mice selected for expression profiling.

Aqp2 RNA was lower in treated mouse kidneys. Aqp2 encodes a water channel downstream of V2 signaling. This exploratory result adds a molecular signal to investigate alongside cyst-fluid secretion and the clinical kidney-volume findings.
