Why the reports are built this way

Nobody has to take our word for any of it.

Every claim in a report links to its source and states its own limit, and the report does not ship until a script confirms that. Open the sources. If the reasoning holds, that is worth something; if it doesn't, you have lost ten minutes.

The gefitinib case is unpaid work on a public asset whose answer is known, so the method can be graded rather than taken on trust.

Where we stop. The gate checks that the work is traceable. It cannot tell you a judgment is right, and no case in our library is expert-signed yet. Disease-specific clinical judgment, endpoint and regulatory strategy need a named reviewer. CMC transfer readiness, tox operations, DMPK, formulation, legal and IP stay data-room and specialist questions, and the reports say so rather than answering them.
