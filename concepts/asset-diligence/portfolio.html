What we've built

Our tools.

Each one gets a defensible answer out of public biomedical data without a month of manual work. DrugAdopt is the one aimed at a whole asset, and the one we sell.

Flagship

DrugAdopt

IND-readiness decision packets from public data

Eight modules mapped to 21 CFR 312.23 and ICH M3(R2), each with its own search log, artifacts, and semantic check. A fail-closed gate refuses to mark the report product-grade until every identifier resolves and every claim matches its source. The output is a decision packet, a gap map, and a verification report bound to a manifest hash and a commit.
