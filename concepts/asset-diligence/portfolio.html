What we've built

The engine didn't come from nowhere.

Before DrugAdopt was a diligence service, it was a set of tools we built for ourselves: agents that query cancer genomics in plain English, rank mouse models, drive spatial analysis, watch a competitive landscape. DrugAdopt is what happens when you point all of it at one asset and put a bioinformatician in front of the output.

Flagship

DrugAdopt

Bioinformatics diligence on a drug asset

Retrieval agents work the literature, trial registries, chemical and target databases, and public omics in parallel. A bioinformatician reads the output, decides what survives, and signs it. You get a report that states what the evidence supports, what it doesn't, and the limit of every source behind it.

The tooling underneath

Agentic tools we built and run.

Each one solves a piece of the same problem: getting a defensible answer out of public biomedical data without a month of manual work. DrugAdopt is the one aimed at a whole asset.

Insight™

Live · Patent pending

Conversational cancer genomics. Ask about TCGA cohorts in plain English and get survival curves, mutation landscapes, and differential expression with the analysis written out. Across 10,000+ patients.

Spatial Advisor

In development

An agent that guides spatial transcriptomics analysis: profiles the data, recommends methods, executes the steps, and adapts as results come back. Cell-type annotation, cross-sample comparison, ligand-receptor analysis.

Mouse Selector

In development

Natural-language search over the JAX mouse database, with ranked matches, explanations for why each model fits, and warnings about the ones that will waste your time.

AI Radar

In development

Automated landscape scanning across seven sources, with scored findings and a weekly read on what moved in your competitive space.
