Scope

What we sign, and what we refer out.

We sign the analysis: whether a computational or biomarker signal is real, mechanism and stratification reasoning, and whether a published claim reproduces. Disease-specific clinical judgment, endpoint design, and regulatory strategy go to a named reviewer.

CMC transfer readiness, tox operations, DMPK, formulation, legal and IP stay data-room and specialist questions. The reports name them as gates rather than answering them.
