What gets worked

Eight axes of risk.

Most assets carry risk on three or four of these. We scope to those, and the report states which questions public data cannot answer.

Mechanism

Target biology, pathway support, expression and dependency evidence across public cohorts.

PK / PD

Exposure, distribution, and whether reported concentrations reach a defensible pharmacology anchor.

Toxicology

Label liabilities, class effects, and whether safety caps the dose below a useful exposure.

SAR

Potency anchors, analogue precedent, and structure-driven arguments for reformulation.

Trial precedent

What has been tried in the indication, how it was designed, how it read out, and why.

Biomarker & stratification

Whether a responder population is definable, and whether the proposed marker survives scrutiny.

RNA-level depth

When the risk is an RNA question (isoform, splicing, modification), we look below the gene-level view where standard analysis stops.

Signal integrity

Whether a reported effect survives batch, composition, and cross-cohort checks, or dissolves under them.

Do the cheap work first

The computer is faster than the bench. Use it in that order.

Every question the public record can already answer is a question you don't need an experiment for. Send us your target and we'll tell you which ones those are, and which ones only your bench can settle. The work you do run is then the work that had to be run.
