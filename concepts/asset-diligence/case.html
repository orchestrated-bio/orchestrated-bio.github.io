Gefitinib failed in unselected NSCLC and worked in EGFR-mutant disease. The lesson was a selection question, so the readout leads with the biomarker matrix and says where the public record stops on each row.

gefitinib · EGFR-mutant NSCLC biomarker strategy matrix · 3 rows
GEF-BMK-001 EGFR exon 19 del / exon 21 L858R predictive · enrichment

Go for biomarker-selected reference use. Stop for unselected restart.

Enriches for canonical sensitizing variants. Uncommon variants need variant-specific evidence rather than a generic activating-mutation claim. PMID 18378519 PMID 19692680

Owner: translational oncology lead

Private-data boundaryPatient-level genotype and outcome tables, and assay chain-of-custody, stay data-room dependencies for a new protocol. Mutation status is a disease-substrate marker, not receptor occupancy.

GEF-BMK-002 Acquired resistance, e.g. EGFR T790M resistance · stratification

Conditional gap for any fresh-development strategy beyond the reference case.

Separates historical EGFR-mutant sensitivity from resistant or modern first-line populations, where osimertinib changes the benchmark. PMID 16923179 PMID 28540278

Owner: clinical strategy lead

Private-data boundaryA rescue strategy needs patient-level resistance genotype, line-of-therapy, and comparator outcomes. Public evidence frames the risk; it cannot support a resistance-selected development claim.

GEF-BMK-003 Exposure-linked EGFR pathway inhibition pharmacodynamic · engagement

Gap for any new regimen or reformulation claim.

Public PK and potency anchors are supportive but are not a measured human PK/PD bridge. No validated phospho-EGFR or occupancy threshold is qualified by public data alone. CHEMBL939 PMID 14578364

Owner: DMPK / translational pharmacology lead

The recommendation

What the report concluded.

Use gefitinib as a historical reference calibration case for labeled EGFR exon 19 deletion and exon 21 L858R selection, while treating current development as conditional on comparator, rights, CMC, assay provenance, and data-room constraints.
