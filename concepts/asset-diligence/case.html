What the evidence would not support.

Prexasertib is a CHK1 inhibitor in platinum-resistant BRCA-wild-type ovarian cancer. The mechanism is plausible and the early single-centre response rates looked strong. The report says the program does not yet rest on a demonstrated tumour vulnerability, and shows the work behind that.

prexasertib · platinum-resistant HGSOC decision packet · 9 rows
H-004 Unselected efficacy clinical

Conditional Do not advance an unselected monotherapy path.

The independent multicentre signal is ORR 11–13%. The single-centre 30–33% results are genuine but non-portable, and cannot be pooled with them. PMID 36192237 PMID 38555285

NextPursue biomarker enrichment, or a randomised comparison against physician's-choice chemotherapy.

H-005 Resistance signal biomarker

Gap No validated resistance mechanism is established.

The replication-machinery candidates (POLA1, POLE, GINS3) are reproducible in direction but FDR-null and same-cohort. The CCNE1 resistance interpretation is null. GSE249587 PMID 38555285

NextFreeze a replication-stress score and test it in an independent cohort with multiplicity control and blinded analysis.

H-003 Tumour exposure PK/PD

Gap The exposure → engagement → response chain cannot be evaluated from public data.

Plasma PK is well characterised and marrow toxicity confirms systemic delivery, but no public data establish that prexasertib reaches tumour CHK1 above the IC50.

NextSponsor tumour PK and a validated pathway-PD readout. Public evidence cannot close this.

H-006 Safety ceiling toxicology

Conditional On-target, largely transient, G-CSF-manageable but not benign.

Grade 3–4 neutropenia across three datasets: 93% (pilot), 85.7% (cohorts 5/6), 37.9% (independent multicentre). PMID 29361470 PMID 36192237

NextA fresh IND needs the nonclinical GLP package; the therapeutic-index question stays open until tumour exposure is measured.

The conclusion

What the report concluded.

CHK1 is a candidate vulnerability in this population and the biology is coherent, but public evidence does not establish a measurable, load-bearing tumour CHK1-dependence mechanism, and no public biomarker supports patient selection today.

Nine rows, each with the experiment that would close it: tumour PK and a validated pathway-PD readout for the exposure gap, a frozen replication-stress score tested in an independent cohort for the resistance gap, biomarker enrichment or a randomised comparison before any unselected monotherapy path.
