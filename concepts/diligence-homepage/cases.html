Selected public reference cases

Three ways diligence changes development.

These are public-evidence teaching cases, not client engagements or claims of prediction. Each names the development decision, the material finding, the action it supports, and the primary records behind it.

Atabecestat · Early Alzheimer disease

Material finding: CSF amyloid lowering did not offset dose-related cognitive worsening, hepatic adverse events, and neuropsychiatric burden.

Decision consequence: target engagement alone is not a viable benefit-risk case. No broad restart without patient-level evidence defining a coherent rescue population.
Primary recordsEARLY phase 2b/3 · PMID 33464300 ↗NCT02569398 results ↗
Decision at stake · broad or selected population?SELECT

Gefitinib · EGFR+ NSCLC

Material finding: in IPASS, progression-free survival favored gefitinib in EGFR-mutant tumors and chemotherapy in EGFR-wild-type tumors.

Decision consequence: the assay-defined population was not supporting detail; it was the development strategy. Stop broad-population claims.
Primary recordsIPASS phase 3 study ↗Prospectively EGFR-mutant phase 3 study ↗
Decision at stake · biomarker-led rescue?STOP

Iniparib · Metastatic TNBC

Material finding: phase 3 did not meet its coprimary PFS and OS criteria, while biochemical work showed iniparib was not a bona fide PARP inhibitor.

Decision consequence: do not transfer PARP-class biomarkers to iniparib without demonstrating PARP-relevant target engagement at plausible exposure.
Primary recordsPhase 3 study ↗Mechanism deconvolution · PMID 22128301 ↗
