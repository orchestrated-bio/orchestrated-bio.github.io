Reference case · Nilotinib / Parkinson disease

The better decision was to hold before another efficacy spend.

When the record is limited to evidence available before 14 December 2020, the disposition is HOLD pending exposure and target-engagement gates. The later randomized report is shown separately as an outcome check—not evidence used to make the call.

  • Decision at the evidence cutoff: hold the current oral formulation—not another efficacy trial.
  • Required before fresh spend: matched plasma/CSF PK, a credible brain-exposure bridge, and human target engagement.
  • Program consequence: reformulate, pursue an analogue, or stop rather than repeat the same development design.

The honest counterfactual: this does not show that the trial would have succeeded. It shows that the formulation should have been redesigned or stopped before the efficacy spend.
