Example front-page decision

Hold the current oral formulation before another efficacy trial.

Nilotinib · Parkinson disease · Public reference case

Why
CNS exposure, human target engagement, response-marker interpretation, and safety margin remain decision-limiting.
Before fresh spend
Require matched plasma/CSF PK, a credible brain-exposure bridge, and a qualified human target-engagement readout.
What changes the call
A reformulation or analogue reaches a declared pharmacology anchor at a tolerable dose and moves the intended CNS pathway.
