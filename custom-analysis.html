Selected work

Two examples.

OPTIC RCC, Phase II

A prospective trial across Cleveland Clinic, University Hospitals, UC Irvine, and UT Southwestern that used an RNA-seq biomarker to assign kidney cancer patients to treatment arms. We built the sample-processing pipeline and patient-data infrastructure, from tissue intake through analysis to results returned to the sites.

Results published in Annals of Oncology, 2025

Desmoplakin cardiomyopathy

Thirty cardiomyocyte RNA-seq samples reanalyzed from public GEO data, with independent fits per cohort and no pooling. Collagen and matrix-remodeling genes moved together in the heterozygote by six months, and two of the separating proteins are secreted, so both can be measured in blood.

Public GEO data, reanalyzed in R
