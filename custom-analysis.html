Where this applies

Scenarios we have worked.

Small-molecule inhibitors

Target engagement, exposure, and selection markers. Our example report works a CHK1 inhibitor in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer.

Antibody-drug conjugates

Payload mechanism and target expression separated, so a marker reflects the biology that drives response rather than the antibody alone.

Antibodies and biologics

Where a target behaves differently by tissue. IL-17A blockade improves psoriasis and worsens Crohn's; both results stay in the report.

Real-world-data biomarkers

Cohorts built from electronic health records and All of Us, with phenotype definitions written down before anything is counted.

We have not worked on cell or gene therapy, and we will say so rather than take the engagement.
