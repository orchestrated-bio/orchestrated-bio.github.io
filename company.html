An engine for finding the right patients.

We build DrugAdopt for small pharma teams with a promising candidate and no spare translational capacity. It grew out of Insight, our cancer-genomics tool, which is still live and under active development.

The report is the visible artifact. The product is the engine underneath it: a reusable set of expert-designed biomarker and bioinformatics workflows that can be adapted from one asset to the next.

Working with your data

Your data does not go to a public model API.

Language models run on private cloud infrastructure we control, not on consumer or third-party model endpoints. Nothing you share is sent to a public API, and nothing is used to train a model.

  • Per-engagement isolation. Each asset is worked in its own environment. Your material is not pooled with another client's, and it is not reused to build anything outside your engagement.
  • You set the boundary. Most of what DrugAdopt does runs on public evidence alone — the example report uses no sponsor data at all. Internal data is optional, and you decide what crosses the line and when.
  • Contracts before data. We sign an NDA before you send anything, and we will work to your MSA, DPA, or security review rather than asking you to accept ours.

Have a specific security requirement? Ask, and we will tell you plainly whether we can meet it.
