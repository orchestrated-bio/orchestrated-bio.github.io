Data handling

Working with your data

Most DrugAdopt work uses public evidence alone. Before you send data, we agree in writing where it will be processed and which outside services, if any, may receive it.

  • Each engagement, whether a DrugAdopt asset or a custom analysis, runs in its own environment. Your material is not pooled with another client's or reused to build anything outside your engagement.
  • Sharing internal data is optional.
  • We sign an NDA before you send anything, and we can work under your MSA, DPA, and security review.
