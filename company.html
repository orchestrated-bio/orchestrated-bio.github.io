Working with your data

Language models run on private cloud infrastructure we control. Data you share is never sent to public or third-party model APIs.

  • Per-engagement isolation. Each DrugAdopt asset is worked in its own environment. Your material is not pooled with another client's, used to train a model, or reused to build anything outside your engagement.
  • You set the boundary. Most of what DrugAdopt does runs on public evidence alone. The example report uses no sponsor data. Internal data is optional, and you decide what to share and when.
  • Contracts before data. We sign an NDA before you send anything, and we will work to your MSA, DPA, or security review rather than asking you to accept ours.

Have a specific security requirement? Ask, and we will tell you plainly whether we can meet it.
