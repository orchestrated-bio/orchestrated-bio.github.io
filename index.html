How the engine builds the case

The report tests four parts of the biomarker case.

DrugAdopt examines clinical response, mechanism, exposure, and safety. Each section states what the public evidence supports, where it stops, and which sources support the finding. If a selection marker is not ready, the report names the experiment needed to test it.

Clinical Phenotype & Endpoints

Every trial to date, its response rate reconciled against an independent benchmark, and which cohorts can and cannot be pooled.

Mechanism & Biomarker Biology

Target biology and candidate biomarkers, worked against public datasets rather than asserted from the label.

Pharmacology & Exposure

What public exposure data can and cannot settle about dose, exposure-response, and target engagement.

Toxicology & Therapeutic Window

The dose-limiting toxicities as they appear across every reported dataset, and which safety questions public data cannot close.

Worked alongside these, and reported where they gate a decision: scope and target product profile, CMC and product quality, protocol design, and the remaining gaps with the experiment that would close each.
