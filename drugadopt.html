What’s in a case

Every angle of your drug, in one evidence package.

DrugAdopt takes one drug in one indication through eleven evidence modules, from disease biology to the patent record. A separate reviewer checks each module, and every finding links back to its source.

Start

Scoping and orientation

We confirm what the drug is, define the question, and map what is publicly known about the drug, disease, trials and datasets before the deeper work begins.

  • Disease and target biology

    Disease frame
    How the disease progresses, and how it is staged and measured.
    Target validation
    Whether the target rationale holds up, and where it stops.
    Dependency
    Whether perturbing the mechanism changes a disease-relevant phenotype in models.

  • Safety and exposure

    Safety
    How mechanistic, nonclinical and human safety evidence compare.
    ADMET and PK/PD
    Whether exposure is plausibly compatible with both activity and safety.

  • Clinic, competition and IP

    Clinical
    Which endpoints have precedent, what they can detect, and whether a trial can enroll.
    Competition
    Who else is developing in the field, and the dated public evidence for each program.
    IP landscape
    Who holds the public patent record, with key term dates. A landscape, not a legal opinion.
Finish

One synthesized report

Findings are joined across modules to answer your question, with the contradictions, open gaps and the data that would close them. You get the evidence; the decisions stay yours.

Tolvaptan in ADPKD
