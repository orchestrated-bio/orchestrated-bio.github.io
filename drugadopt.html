DrugAdopt

Public evidence and gaps for your asset.

DrugAdopt examines public data on one preclinical asset in one indication, including evidence gaps and the measurements that could address them.

  • Findings linked to sources

    Findings name their measures and link to the publications, datasets or registry records behind them.

  • Evidence gaps and search limits

    Where evidence is missing, the report says what was searched and which measurement, dataset or trial could address the question.

  • New analyses of public data

    We reanalyze public expression and genomic data with documented methods and deliver the code with the result.

  • The decisions stay yours

    We present evidence and judgments you can check. We do not recommend advancing, funding or licensing an asset.

What’s in a case

Evidence on one asset in one indication.

DrugAdopt examines the topics below, including disease biology and patent records. A separate reviewer screens each section. Findings link to their sources.

Start

Scoping and orientation

We establish the drug’s identity and the question to address. We then review public information on the drug, disease, trials and datasets.

  • Disease and target biology

    Disease biology
    How the disease progresses, and how it is staged and measured.
    Target evidence
    Evidence supporting or challenging the target rationale.
    Dependency
    Whether perturbing the mechanism changes a disease-relevant phenotype in models.
    Biomarkers
    Candidate target-engagement and response markers, and the public data behind each.

  • Safety and exposure

    Safety
    How mechanistic, nonclinical and human safety evidence compare.
    ADMET and PK/PD
    Whether exposure is plausibly compatible with both activity and safety.

  • Clinic, competition and IP

    Clinical
    Endpoint precedent and sensitivity, with public evidence on trial enrollment.
    Competition
    Other programs in development and the dated public evidence for each.
    IP landscape
    Public patent records and their listed holders and term dates. This is not a legal opinion.
Finish

One report

The report brings findings from the sections together to address your question. It describes contradictions and unresolved questions, with data that could help resolve them.

example case study

Tolvaptan in ADPKD

We reanalyzed public kidney-expression data from tolvaptan-treated and untreated Pkd1-knockout mice and found lower RNA for Aqp2, a water channel downstream of tolvaptan’s V2 target. The case study traces that finding from the report to the slides, spreadsheet and code behind it.
