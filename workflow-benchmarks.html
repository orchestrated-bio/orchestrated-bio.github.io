Benchmark results

Cohort selection and source links

GPT-6 Luna received identical staged evidence and questions in two conditions. The guided condition added about 100 words of analyst instructions, including a request to cite the supplied sources. Each condition ran once per study. An exact request had the reference sample pairs in the correct direction.

CheckPlain requestWritten guidance
Exact comparison35 of 5139 of 51
At least one supplied source link in the response3 of 5149 of 51

Fourteen of the 51 studies had been used in development. The paired change in exact requests was 7.8 percentage points. The study-resampled 95% interval was −5.9 to +21.6 points. The source-link count measures whether a link appeared. Expert review of the linked evidence is a separate check.

Sample faults

In a synthetic count panel, the workflow flagged 20 of 20 within-pair label reversals and 20 of 20 swaps between donors. In modified copies of one real liver-tumor dataset, the main quality flags detected 0 of 10 swaps between patients. A separate sex-marker check detected 3 of those 10. The nearest-sample check was inactive in that tumor comparison. The same fault can be detectable in one study design and much harder to detect in another. Public liver-tumor study
